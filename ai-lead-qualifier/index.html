The Problem

Your sales team is chasing
the wrong leads

Every inbound lead looks the same in your inbox. Without qualification, your team spends the same energy on a curious student as on a CFO ready to sign a contract.

Slow, manual triage

Sales reps read every lead by hand to decide if it's worth a call. That takes time - and warm leads go cold while you're sorting.

No prioritisation

Without a scoring system, high-value prospects get the same generic follow-up as someone who was just browsing. Conversion suffers.

Inconsistent follow-up

Different reps send different messages. Some leads hear back in minutes, others wait days. AI guarantees a consistent, personalised response every time.

The Solution

From form submission to scored lead
in three steps

Step 1

Lead fills in the form

A prospect submits your website contact form - name, company, and a short description of what they need. That's all the AI requires.

Step 2

AI scores the lead

AI analyses the message, evaluates intent, budget signals, and urgency - then classifies the lead as high or low intent with a confidence score and reasoning.

Step 3

Personalised follow-up sent

High-intent leads get a warm, direct follow-up email. Low-intent leads receive a nurturing sequence. The lead is stored in Supabase and appears in your dashboard - scored and ready to act on.

How it works

AI automation preview

Form + live AI scoring

The prospect fills in a short form. The moment they submit, the right panel shows the AI-generated qualification report - score, reasoning, strengths, concerns, and a recommended next step. All within two seconds.

Automated routing by intent

Every submission is automatically scored and routed. High-intent leads get a priority follow-up; low-intent leads enter a nurturing sequence. No manual sorting required.

Transparent AI reasoning

The report doesn't just show a number - it explains why. Strengths, concerns, and a concrete next step, so your sales team knows exactly how to approach each lead.
