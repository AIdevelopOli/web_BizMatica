FAQ

Questions we get
most often

How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on scope: how many systems we connect, the volume of data, and whether you want ongoing support. After a free audit you get a fixed quote, usually within 48 hours. No open-ended hourly rates.

We don’t have technical people. Can we still do this?

The technical part is our job. You describe the process that slows you down; we design, build, and deploy the solution. Your role is to provide cooperation, system access, and feedback.

Who maintains the solution and what if it breaks?

After launch we monitor and maintain the automation so it runs reliably and adapts as your business changes. If something fails, we handle it.

Do we have to switch the tools we already use?

Usually not. The automation connects to your existing systems such as CRM, email, accounting, or e-shop via their APIs. The goal is to fit into what you already have.

What happens to our data?

We build solutions so your data stays under your control. We use vetted providers and process only the data a given automation needs. The exact data flow and GDPR compliance are documented during the solution design.

How long does deployment take?

Most automations are deployed in 1–3 weeks depending on complexity. A free audit clarifies scope and timeline before any commitment.
