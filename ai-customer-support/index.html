How it works

From inbox to draft reply
in three steps

Step 1

Customer reaches out

A customer sends a support email or types a question in the chat widget on your website. Both channels are handled automatically.

Step 2

AI finds the answer

The AI searches your knowledge base - FAQs, policies, manuals - and retrieves the most relevant information.

Step 3

Answer delivered

Chat customers get an instant reply in seconds. Email customers receive a professional draft in your inbox - ready to review and send. Fully automatic sending is also available, depending on your workflow.
