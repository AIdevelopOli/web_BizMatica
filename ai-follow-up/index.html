The Problem

Your sales team
is dropping follow-ups

Hot leads go cold because reps are too busy. Generic templates get ignored. And nobody knows who replied and who's still waiting. That's exactly what this system fixes.

Forgotten follow-ups

A rep sends the first email and moves on. The hot prospect reaches out to a competitor - and the deal that could have been yours isn't.

Generic templates

The same email to everyone doesn't work. Recipients spot a template and ignore it. Personalization lifts reply rates by 31% on average - but you can't scale that manually.

No visibility

Who replied? Who opened but didn't respond? Who's still waiting for a follow-up? Without a system, that data lives scattered across individual inboxes.

The Solution

From new lead to reply
in three steps

Step 1

AI writes the first contact

As soon as a new qualified lead arrives, AI reads their profile - name, company, segment, score - and writes a personalized email in 3 seconds. Consultant tone, not a sales pitch.

Step 2

System monitors replies

The email is sent automatically via Gmail. Every morning, the system checks for replies. If the lead responded, the sequence stops automatically - the lead is handed to a sales rep.

Step 3

Automatic follow-up

No reply after 3 days? AI writes follow-up #2. After 4 more days, follow-up #3. Each email is different - it references the previous message and keeps a natural conversational tone.

How it works

System demo

Sequence dashboard - real-time overview

Set up the campaign once. The system handles timing, tracks the status of every email, and shows you metrics - open rate, reply rate, and active sequence count.

Smart sequence branching

When a reply arrives, the sequence stops automatically and the lead is handed off. No follow-up ever fires after a customer has already replied.

Personalized AI email

Every email is written individually - AI factors in name, company, segment, and previous interaction. The recipient feels genuine interest, not automation.
